      Matches the game exactly: glyph shapes and widths come from power green small.ttf at its in-game size, wrapping follows Essentials' getLineBrokenChunks, and layout follows Pokedex Entry Text.rb.

      A line holds 440 px of text, and a space is 6 px. Entries can be up to 5 lines. Text past line 5 is not shown in game. Entries with 4 or 5 lines are centred.
